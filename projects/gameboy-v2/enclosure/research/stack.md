# stack

## Summary
Z frame: board bottom z 0, board top z 1.6, +z toward the screen. Over the 2090 (board x 54.6-135.9) the stack is fixed by parts already on the board. The 8.5 mm sockets (top z 10.1) plus the ~2.5 mm male-header body (estimated) put the 2090 PCB at z 12.6-14.17 and its glass top at z 18.27 (allow 18.6). The 2090's back-side parts reach down to z 9.95: the original revision's 2.65 mm SOIC-20W level shifters, directly over the cell. Everything else is low: mainboard parts under the module reach z 7.1 at most (J8), the top edge 10.1 (J1 SWD header), the grips 4.95 (SKRT side buttons), and the tact-switch tops are at z 3.1. Below the board: the BT830 reaches z -1.7 (left grip), the J3/J4 socket pin tails z -1.6 (not modelled in board.json), and there is nothing under the right grip. Minimum shell inner faces with 0.3 mm clearance, front / back: centre z 18.6 / -1.9; left grip 5.25 / -2.0; right grip 5.25 / -0.3; top edge 10.4 / -1.7; bottom edge 7.5 over the cell (16.2 over the 2090 ear ends) / -2.0. With a 1.5 mm bezel and a 1.6 mm floor the device is 23.7 mm thick over the 2090 (z -3.6 to 20.1), 0.8 mm under the GBA's 24.5. 2.0 mm walls give 24.6, and a separate 1 mm window gives 24.7. The GBA's grip taper cannot be copied on the back, because the board fills the grips to 2.5 mm from the outline. A flat GBA-style front at z 20.1 makes the grips 22-24 mm thick (the GBA is 11-12.5 at the ends and about 18-21 at the D-pad/A). It also needs about 19 mm of cap plus stem per button, and puts the L/R, volume, power and USB-C (z 2.3-3.3) about 17 mm behind the front face.

Battery: its top must stay at or below z 8.8 (swell room plus clearance under the 2.65 mm parts), so with 8.5 mm of cell its bottom must be at or below z 0.3. In other words it has to sink through the cut-out. Rest it on the back-shell floor at z -2.0, which puts its top at 6.5 with 3.45 mm of headroom. Hold it with removable foam tape plus 1.2 mm fences in the 2 mm cut-out gap, and face the PCM/lead end toward J8. enclosure.py currently places it at z 2.0-10.5, which overlaps the original-revision 2090 parts by 0.55 mm.

Speaker: seal its Ø23.4 front lip to the front-shell inner face through 0.5 mm foam (about z 13.4-18.3 with a flat front). Hold it up with a cup from the back shell, so the 50 mm leads soldered to J5 are never pulled when the front shell comes off. enclosure.py has it at board level, which leaves a ~12 mm air column in front of it.

LRA: it sits over the board, so glue it flat to the front-shell inner face. Mounting it on the back would need a 1.55 mm pocket in a 1.6 mm floor.

Fasteners: there are 4 shell screws, the 3 board holes plus draw_mech's off-board bottom-right screw at (140.5, 100.5), which board.json and enclosure.py are missing. Use M2x6 screws from the back into M2x3 brass heat-set inserts in the front shell. The inserts sit at the tips of Ø6 front-shell bosses that run about 17 mm down to the board top and need gussets. Merge the two top bosses into the top wall, and D-cut the top-right one where it faces J1, which is only 3.3 mm away. Back bosses are Ø6 x 2.0 mm with a Ø2.4 hole and a Ø4.2 x 2.0 counterbore, and the board is clamped between the two bosses. Self-tapping screws (Ø5 boss, Ø1.6-1.7 pilot, M2x8-10) are only good enough for fit prints, since the shells come off for SWD (front) and the test points (back). 2090: four M2.5 female-female hex standoffs, 11.0 mm long (8.5 + 2.5; measure first and never go shorter), 4.5 mm across flats. Use M2.5x4 screws at both ends, with low heads under the board (no more than 1.4 mm tall).

## Risks
- enclosure.py mech() puts the cell at z 2.0-10.5: line 57 treats pcb_bottom_above_board = 11.0 as an absolute z. That overlaps the original-revision 2090's SOIC-20W level shifters (bottom z 9.95) by 0.55 mm, leaves 0.10 mm to the rev-D EYESPI connector, and leaves the cell floating 4 mm above a z -2.0 floor. Move it to z -2.0..6.5.
- A flat GBA-style front has to sit at z 20.1 or higher because of the 2090 glass (z 18.27). The board and BT830 fill the grips to 2.5 mm from the outline, so the GBA's rear taper can't be copied. The grips come out 22-24 mm thick (the GBA is 11-21 there), each keycap needs about 19 mm of cap plus stem, and the L/R, volume and power controls (z 2.3-3.3) sit about 17 mm behind the face. Decide between accepting this, tapering the front, or stepping the front before designing the front shell.
- Every 2090 height above the sockets depends on the estimated 2.5 mm male-header body, plus the panel's 4.0 +/-0.2 and a 0.3 mm FPC at the JP2 end. The glass top could be 0.3-0.6 mm higher than nominal. Measure the plugged-in module (mainboard top to PCB bottom and to glass top) before printing the bezel or buying standoffs.
- If a 2090 standoff is shorter than the real seated gap, tightening its screws bows the module and loads its 40 header joints. Buy 11 mm only after measuring, and shim with washers rather than going shorter.
- The 4th shell screw (bottom-right, off-board at (140.5, 100.5), draw_mech.py SCREWS_MOVED) is not in board.json. As a result enclosure.py builds only 3 bosses. Export it or hard-code it.
- The top-right shell-screw boss is about 3.3 mm from J1's 8.5 mm SWD header body. A Ø6 insert boss leaves about 0.3 mm (a Ø5 boss about 0.8). Offset it into the top wall or D-cut it.
- A back floor at z -2.0 leaves about 0.25-0.3 mm under the BT830 (1.6 +/-0.15, so down to -1.75), 0.4 mm under the J3/J4 socket pin tails (-1.6, not modelled in board.json because the sockets are top-side THT), and 0.25 mm under M2.5 pan-head standoff screws (-1.75). Do not raise the floor there; use low-head M2.5 screws or local reliefs.
- 5 mm across-flats M2.5 hex standoffs come within about 0.3 mm of the J3/J4 socket ends and the 2090's header body. Use 4.5 mm across flats or orient a flat toward the socket.
- Speaker: at board level (as in enclosure.py now) it sits behind a ~12 mm air column under a flat front's grille, which hurts treble and adds a resonance. If it is glued into the front shell instead, its 50 +/-3 mm leads hard-soldered to J5 limit how far the front shell can lift (~20-25 mm). Cradle it from the back shell and foam-seal it to the front.
- LRA: putting it on the back shell needs a 1.55 mm pocket in a 1.6 mm floor (or a bump on the outside). Glue it to the front-shell inner face; its ~100 mm leads leave plenty of slack. enclosure.py currently models it sitting on the board.
- Thickness margin against the GBA's 24.5 is only 0.8 mm. 2.0 mm walls (24.6) or a separate 1 mm window over the 2090 glass (24.7) already exceed it.
- The 111.6 mm bottom edge between the two bottom screws has no fastener, so printed shells are likely to gap there. Add snaps, or a wall-merged boss at the bottom centre (4.25 mm between the cell and the wall).
- The user's 2090 revision is unknown. The original revision's 2.65 mm SOIC-20W parts sit directly over the cell, so size the cell's headroom for 2.65 mm, not rev D's 2.0.
- Some numbers are not from a rendered datasheet. J1's tail length is assumed to be 3.0 mm (its 3D model is missing). The LRA PDF's images fail to render, so its values come from the markdown transcription with truncated tolerances. The heat-set insert OD/hole/wall are typical vendor values (the vendor table is an image). Print a test coupon.
- The ~17 mm front-shell bosses and long keycap guides are slender. They will flex when inserts are heat-set and under button load: gusset them to walls or ribs, and set the inserts with the shell face-down.
