# exterior

## Summary
The outer form is a flat 24.0 mm GBA-silhouette slab, split at the board's top face. I test-built it in build123d 0.13, using scratch files only.

Frames: enclosure X = board x, Y = -board y. z: board bottom 0, board top 1.6. GBA frame to board mm: left half (x, y) + (20, 30); right half + (26, 30); centred items + (23, 30). The widening S is 6.0.

1. Outline. Use board.json mech.silhouette exactly as the raw 54-point polygon. After the Y flip the loop runs clockwise, so the face normal points -Z. Always extrude with dir=(0,0,1).
2. Back face plane: z = -3.6. That is the tallest bottom part (U12 BT830, 1.70) + 0.3 clearance + 1.6 floor, the same value today's fit_frame uses.
3. Front face plane: z = +20.4. That is the board top (1.6) + the 2090 glass top (16.7 above the board, z 18.27) + a 0.5 gasket gap + a 1.6 skin. Use 20.9 if you want exactly the GBA's 24.5 and room for a 1 mm lens or D-pad dish recess.
4. Edges. Fillet the front and back perimeter edges R3. The inner cavity is offset(-2.0), extruded from z -2.0 to 18.8, with its edges filleted R1.4. Both shells come out valid, minimum wall 1.600 mm, board clearance 0.495 mm. R2 with no inner fillet also builds. A back edge of R4 and a spline through the vertices both fail.
5. Split plane: z = +1.6 (board top face). The front shell is 18.8 tall and prints face-down; the back shell is 5.2 tall and prints back-down. Every edge-facing part sits above the split, so each opening is a notch in the front shell's rim:
   - USB-C: z 1.6–4.86.
   - L/R and volume side switches: actuator axis at z 3.3.
   - Power slide: z 1.6–3.0.
   The GBA's own seam is 7.3 mm behind its front face, through the middle of its L/R band. Ours is reversed because the board is at the back.
6. Grip taper. The GBA keeps its front flat and thins the back to 14.5–22.2 mm over the outer ~25 mm of each grip. With our front at 20.4, that taper would cut 3–12 mm into the board stack at board x < 45 and x > 145.5. So the grips stay 24 mm thick, 3–10 mm thicker than the GBA's. The grips themselves only need ~9–11 mm, so a lower, stepped button deck is the only sensible thin-grip variant; it would not look like a GBA.
7. Front features, in board mm:
   - Screen window: 58.6 x 44.2 at (92.61, 71.00).
   - Decorative GBA lens: re-centred with (x + 20.36, y + 32.63). This leaves a 12.1 mm logo band, where the GBA prints "GAME BOY ADVANCE".
   - Speaker grille: the GBA's 5 tilted slots, re-centred on the speaker at (151.45, 85.10).
   - LED window: Ø2.5 at D1 (144.14, 44.70), exactly the GBA's power-LED spot.
   - D-pad, A, B, Start and Select openings: at the switch centres.
   - L/R: along the outline's top corners, from the notch vertices (24.53, 44.16) and (165.97, 44.16) to (53.14, 31.67) and (137.36, 31.67).
8. Back. The GBA battery-door region (60.7 x 37.6 at (95.44, 89.5)) is smaller than our cell (62 x 38.5 at (95.25, 86.5)). There is no cartridge slot, because the USB-C and SWD header occupy the top edge.

Scratch files are in /tmp/encl_outer: outer_test.py, wall_check.py, geom.py, taper.py and the patent sheets D00002.png, D3a.png, D1b.png, zu.png. No repo file was changed.

## Risks
- Looks and ergonomics: a 24 mm slab reads as a GBA from the front but not from the side. The grips are 3-10 mm thicker than the GBA's, because its back taper would cut into our board in the outer ~25 mm of each grip (GBA x < 25).
- Long button and light-path stems: the board top is 18.8 mm behind the front face (the GBA's is roughly 6-7 mm). D-pad, A/B and Start/Select caps need ~19 mm guided plungers, and the D1 light pipe is ~18 mm long. The only way to shorten them is a stepped lower button deck, which drops the GBA-flat front.
- L/R and volume buttons: their actuators (z 3.3) are only ~0.5 mm (L/R) and ~1 mm (volume) from the 2.0 mm wall's inner face. The buttons must replace or pass through the wall. Our L/R band also sits near the back (10-19 mm behind the face) rather than the GBA's 3-12 mm.
- Glass-to-skin gap: 0.5 mm against roughly +/-0.3 mm of module stack tolerance (panel 4.0 +/-0.2, PCB, and the 2.5 mm header body, which is an estimate). Measure the assembled 2090 height before fixing z_front; use 20.7-20.9 if it comes out tall.
- Back plane -3.6 assumes nothing under the board taller than U12 (1.70). Screw heads for the 2090 standoffs if driven from below (M2.5 pan head ~1.75-2.0) or any insert hardware would need floor relief or a lower back. The BT830 antenna side should stay free of metal.
- enclosure.py mech() line 57: cell_top = T + 11.0 - T - 0.5 = 10.5 puts the cell at z 2.0-10.5. That overlaps the original-rev 2090's SOIC-20W (bottom at z ~9.95) and is within ~0.1 mm of rev D's EYESPI connector. Resting the cell on the back floor (z -2.0..6.5) clears both. Without a fix, the viewer shows a false fit.
- Fillets are fragile on this outline. Keep the raw polygon with R3 edges and R1.4 inner fillets (tested). R4 at the back, a periodic spline (0.75 mm overshoot, 0.44 mm board clearance) and an approximating spline all fail or distort. Smoothing the polygon in 2D needs re-checking against the 2.5 mm board inset.
- FDM printing: both R3 perimeter edges are on the bed and will droop slightly (~0.6 mm). Large recesses on the bed faces (lens recess, back-door panel) print as bridges. Prefer chamfers, V-grooves, through-cuts or flush multi-colour first layers.
- Screen frame asymmetry is unavoidable: the 2090's window is 2.64 mm left of the device centre. A GBA lens frame is either off-centre on the body (centred on the window) or holds the window off-centre (4.8 vs 10.1 mm borders).
- Speaker acoustics: the grille's open area is 18-23%. If the speaker sits at board level (z ~1.6-6.5) there is a ~12 mm air column to the grille; mounting it against the front skin couples it better. The B cap is 2.1 mm from the speaker body in plan, which limits the cap's retention flange on that side.
- Lap joint at the split must be interrupted wherever the rim is cut. The USB-C overhangs the board edge by ~1.4 mm (into inset 1.09), and every button or slider notch also breaks the rim.
- Measurement confidence: the GBA edge radii and seam depth come from design-patent line drawings. The depth axis is normalised, about 2% over-scale by line centres. Treat them as +/-0.5 mm. The FIG.5 radius is an envelope, i.e. the tightest section along the edge.
