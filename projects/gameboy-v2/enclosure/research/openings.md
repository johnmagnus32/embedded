# openings

## Summary
Read-only research; nothing in the repo was changed. Scratch files are in /tmp/gb2-enc: the scripts rays.py, wallcheck.py, mechcheck.py and fp_dump.py, the datasheet renders, and the USB-IF Type-C spec. Coordinates are in the enclosure frame (X right, Y = -KiCad y, Z up, board top at Z=1.6).

Wall clearance: only the USB-C receptacle J7 actually enters the 2 mm wall. Every other board part clears the inner wall by at least 0.8 mm. The switch SW1 appears to intrude only because board.json uses a bounding box for it.

Openings in the wall:

- **USB-C J7.**
  - The mouth is centred at X 95.25, Z 3.25 (1.65 mm above the board top).
  - Mouth 8.34 x 2.56; shell 8.94 x about 3.2, top at Z 4.86.
  - The receptacle face is at Y -31.09: 1.41 mm past the board edge and 0.91 mm into the wall, with 1.09 mm of wall in front of it.
  - The USB-C spec allows the outer surface at most about 0.40 mm in front of the receptacle face, so a plug-sized hole isn't enough.
  - Cut a 9.5 x 3.8 stadium pocket through the wall (Z 1.35-5.15), plus a 13 x 7 overmold recess (Z -0.25 to 6.75) 1.1 mm deep, i.e. flush with the receptacle face.
  - J7's 3D model in KiCad is 2.22 mm off, so use the pads, not the model.
- **Power slide SW1.**
  - The lever is 1.3 wide x 1.0 thick (Z 1.8-2.8), travels 1.6 mm, and runs along the 13.36° bottom-left wall.
  - Its tip (enc (42.65,-100.87)) is 0.97 mm short of the inner wall and 2.97 mm from the outside, too deep and small for a finger. It needs a captive printed slider in a 4.0 x 2.2 slot (Z 1.6-3.8) centred on (41.96,-103.76).
  - ON appears to be toward the west (pin 1); check with a meter. This matches the GBA's switch position.
- **Volume SW10/SW11 and shoulders SW12/SW13.**
  - All four are Ø2 stems at Z 3.3 with 0.2 mm travel, each axis exactly normal to the wall. Each stem tip is 0.82 mm short of the inner wall and 2.82 mm from the outer surface.
  - Outer-surface axis points: SW10 (167.97,-53.98), SW11 (168.94,-61.98), SW12 (37.13,-33.61), SW13 (153.33,-33.60).
  - Suggested cut-outs: 4.0 x 3.6 per plunger (Z 1.5-5.1), or one 13 x 3.6 volume rocker slot centred at (168.45,-57.98).
  - The L/R stems sit at the centre of the GBA shoulder envelopes, but about 5-13 mm lower than the GBA's L/R band.

Access points and cabling:

- **SWD header J1** (pins at Y -37, X 118.5-128.7, tips at Z 10.1): no access with the front shell on. A Dupont plug's cable would exit through the front face. Since SWD is the only way to flash, a front-face hatch of about 13.4 x 3.4 (X 116.9-130.3) is worth it; keep it west of the top-right screw boss at X 130.7.
- **Speaker and LRA pads (J5, J6):** through-hole pads 3.3 mm and 2.5 mm from their parts. The 50 mm speaker leads and about 100 mm LRA leads leave plenty of slack, and no openings are needed.
- **Battery connector J8:** it sits under the 2090 and takes the plug from the south. Put the cell's lead end to the east and run the roughly 100 mm leads up the J4 strip. There's about 65 mm of slack to stow and a tight bend at the plug.
- **Back test points:** these are for bring-up, so taking the back shell off is fine. An optional 11 x 3 console hatch at (91.69,-63.9) only pays off with routine UART use, and the console pitch isn't uniform (2.29 / 2.54 / 2.54).

Also found:

- The power LED D1 at (144.14,-44.70) sits exactly at the GBA's LED window, so it needs a front window and a light pipe of about 17 mm.
- A GBA-position speaker grille would sit 4.75 mm off our speaker; centre it on the speaker instead.
- The 2090's microSD is unused, so no card slot is needed.
- The top-left and top-right screw bosses merge with the wall.

## Risks
- USB-C: the receptacle face is 1.09 mm behind the outer surface, and the USB-C spec allows at most about 0.40 mm. Without the outer overmold relief (13 x 7, about 1.1 mm deep), overmolded plugs seat about 0.64 mm short, which can mean intermittent contact or no latch. The relief leaves only 0.91 mm of wall around the receptacle pocket.
- Don't size the USB-C pocket from KiCad's 3D view or STEP: J7's 3D model sits 2.22 mm inboard of its true position.
- SW1: the lever ends 0.97 mm short of the inner wall, is only 1.0 x 1.3 mm and travels 1.6 mm, so a plain slot is not usable by hand and a captive printed slider is needed. The ON direction is read from the drawing's circuit diagram and should be checked with a meter. board.json has a bounding-box courtyard and a default height (1.5 instead of 1.40) for SW1.
- Side and shoulder switches: the stems end 0.82 mm short of the inner wall with only 0.2 mm travel. Caps need tight guiding and a thin captive flange (0.82 mm gap to the stem tip). A GBA-look L/R cap would sit about 5-13 mm higher than the stems (Z 3.3) and needs a lever arm, and the D1 light pipe sits just inside the R-shoulder envelope.
- J1 hatch margin: the top-right shell-screw boss starts 0.76 mm east of J1's body; a wider hatch or moved boss is needed for comfortable Dupont insertion. The Dupont housing size (about 12.7 x 2.5 x 14) is an estimate.
- Battery: about 100 mm leads (PKCELL drawing; the Adafruit 2011 cable length is unverified) leave about 65 mm of slack. The mated JST plug tail ends about 3 mm from the cell, so the bend is tight. J8 is under the 2090, so the cell can't be unplugged without removing the module.
- Parting plane: if the shell splits at the board top (Z 1.6, as the placeholder tray does), the receptacle shell bottom sits only 0.05 mm above the tray rim, so relieve the rim by about 0.3 mm. The overmold relief spans both halves. The SW1, volume and shoulder openings are best made as notches open at the parting plane so sliders and caps drop in before closing.
- Bottom-side parts in the left grip (C50 at 1.13 mm, C47, C49, U12 BT830) are close to the inner wall. Any GBA-style rounding of the back edges must keep at least 0.3 mm clearance there.
- The console test points are not on a uniform 2.54 pitch (TP9 to TP11 is 2.29), which affects a pogo-strip hatch.
- LRA lead length (about 100 mm) comes from a partly garbled markdown extraction; the VG0832022D PDF is corrupt. The USB-C mouth height (1.65 ±0.05) is derived from the VRML and drawing scale, not printed on the datasheet.
