# gb2 pre-fab footprint checklist

Generated by `fab/fab.py` from gb2-bom.csv, BOM.md and the board. Wrong footprints, not routing, are what
usually kills a first spin, so check each line against the part's datasheet before ordering. The fastest way:
print the F.Fab / B.Fab layers 1:1 (KiCad: File > Print, 1:1 scale, check a 10 mm dimension with a ruler) and lay
the real connectors, switches, the 2090 module and the battery on the paper.

Highest risk first. LCSC links go to the part page (datasheet PDF linked there); local copies in `cad/docs/`.

## Assembly files (fab/out/, check in JLC's online preview)

- **Rotations:** easyeda2kicad footprints come from the same EasyEDA library JLC's placement uses, so most need no
  offset - but check every polarized part (ICs, diodes, the LED, Q1, the crystal) in JLC's 3D/2D preview,
  and especially the 35 **bottom-side** parts (rotation conventions differ most there).
- **Board:** 4 layers, 1.6 mm, stackup **JLC04161H-7628** (the I2C / impedance estimates assume it); 1 oz outer;
  min track/space 0.15/0.15 mm; vias 0.45/0.25 mm tented; ENIG recommended (0.5 mm-pitch QFN/LQFP/TSSOP flatness).
- **Double-sided assembly** (back: MCU / FPGA decap rings, charger parts). Hand-fit after: J1, J3, J4 (sockets),
  U12 (BT830, back), J5 speaker, J6 LRA wires.
